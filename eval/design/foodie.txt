Design a database for "Foodie", a food delivery company. Customers have an id, name, phone and address. Restaurants have an id, name and area. Every restaurant offers several menu items, each with an id, item name and price. Riders have an id, name and vehicle type. A customer places orders; each order has an id, order date, status, the rider who delivers it and the restaurant it comes from. One order can contain many menu items and one menu item can appear in many orders, with a quantity for each.
